# GSM Research Lab

{: .no_toc }

**GSM Research Lab** is the non-normative GSM research product. Systemics is
the paradigm. GSM is a candidate systemics model under test.

Research records evidence, hypotheses, unresolved tensions, and candidate
outcomes. It does not inherit semantic constraints merely because they appear
in normative artifacts. Hypotheses remain non-normative unless and until
accepted through the GSM Change Proposal process.

Current version: **v1.0.0-beta.1**.
