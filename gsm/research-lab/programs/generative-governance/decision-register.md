## Status vocabulary

These statuses are not research record roles.

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Active | On the critical path; a next test is identified. |
| Parked | Listed, but not on the current test path. |
| Hypothesis | Bound to a numbered `H-` record under investigation. |
| Proposed | A GSM Change Proposal carries the decision. |
| Accepted | The proposal was accepted for normative integration. |
| Rejected | The candidate change will not proceed. |
| Merged | Closed into another decision ID. |

## Resolution requirements

Each resolved decision must record:

1. selected outcome and rejected alternatives;
2. systemics rationale;
3. baseline evidence, observations, counterexamples, and falsifiers;
4. selected placement in core GSM, the GSM ontology, or a derived view;
5. selected normative semantics and affected GSM artifacts;
6. conformance requirements added, changed, or withdrawn;
7. dependencies on other decision IDs.
