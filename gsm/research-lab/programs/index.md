This section holds non-normative research into GSM semantics. Systemics is
the paradigm. GSM is a candidate systemics model under test. Research records
evidence, hypotheses, unresolved tensions, and candidate outcomes. It does
not inherit semantic constraints merely because they appear in normative
artifacts.

## Status vocabulary

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Exploratory | The problem and candidate semantics are still being investigated. |
| Hypothesis | A testable semantic proposition under investigation. |
| Change proposal | A GSM Change Proposal is under the process defined in `GOVERNANCE.md`. |
| Accepted | The proposal was accepted and is awaiting or undergoing normative integration. |
| Rejected | The proposal was considered and will not be integrated. |

Every research document must identify its status. Hypotheses remain
non-normative unless and until accepted through the GSM Change Proposal process.

## Research programs

### Archetype referential model

[Archetype Referential Model](archetype-referential-model.md) investigates
namespaced identity, governed versioning, version-pinned references, static
composition, and lifecycle eligibility as one connected contract.

### Generative governance

[Generative Governance](generative-governance/) is a governed research program
with published quality criteria, numbered records, a case corpus, and a
decision register:

1. [Causal Model](generative-governance/causal-model.md) - organization,
   purpose, function, and viability evaluations.
2. [Governance Chain](generative-governance/governance-chain.md) -
   regulation, feedback, variety, and operational closure.
3. [DNA Production](generative-governance/dna-production.md) - autopoietic
   production of DNA and lifecycle handoff.
4. [Cases](generative-governance/cases.md) - executed discriminating examples.
5. [Decision Register](generative-governance/decision-register.md) - stable IDs
   for unresolved decisions shared across the dossier.

## Promotion into GSM

Substantive changes follow the GSM Change Proposal process in
[`GOVERNANCE.md`](https://github.com/poesis-cloud/gsm-specifications/blob/main/GOVERNANCE.md).
A proposal must state its motivation, selected semantics, affected normative
artifacts, and conformance requirements.

Implementation-specific experiments and impact analyses remain in their
respective processor repositories. Reference implementations may retain
temporary companion copies while active work depends on them; those copies are
not normative GSM sources.
