# GSM Ontology

{: .no_toc }

> **In construction.** This product's documentation is being written.

**GSM Ontology** are domain, standard, and legal vocabularies sourced into GSM and ready to enforce. Each framework is expressed with GSM's own primitives — Archetypes that carry the vocabulary and semantics of the source model, and Directives and Norms that carry its normative content — so a published standard or regulation becomes machine-evaluable governance instead of prose.

Sourced framework families include enterprise-architecture models (TOGAF), quality and data-quality standards (ISO 25010, ISO 25012), regulations (GDPR, NIS2, DORA), provenance vocabulary (PROV), and security content automation (SCAP).
