## Milestones
{: .no_toc }

- **Foundation** — shipped in the product's current 0.x (implemented today).
- **Near-term** — the product's next minor (P0, feasibility now).
- **Mid-term** — a later 0.x minor (P1, feasibility next).
- **Future** — a post-GA 1.x batch (P2–P3, later).
- **Exploratory** — research-tier; unversioned — may evolve substantially or not ship.

Milestones express prioritization and implementation reality — **not delivery commitments or dates**. The canonical vocabulary, version semantics, and per-product version milestones live on the **[Milestones & Versions]({% link milestones.md %})** page.
